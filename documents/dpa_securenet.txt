DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (DPA)

Parties:
Controller: Global Retail AG
Processor (Vendor): SecureNet Systems GmbH

Effective Date: 5 February 2026
Term: Coterminous with the underlying Services Agreement.

Contract Value: Included within the master services fees (no separate charge).

Subject Matter:
Processing of customer contact data on behalf of the Controller to provide
email delivery and support-ticket services.

GDPR Compliance:
Processor processes personal data only on documented instructions from the
Controller, in full compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). Processor
maintains a record of processing activities, applies encryption in transit and
at rest, and ensures staff are bound by confidentiality.

Sub-processors:
No new sub-processor is engaged without prior written authorisation and an
equivalent DPA. A current list of sub-processors is maintained and shared.

Data Subject Rights & Breach:
Processor assists the Controller with data-subject requests and notifies the
Controller of any personal data breach without undue delay and within 48 hours.

Data Location:
All personal data is stored within the EU/EEA. No transfers outside the EEA
occur without Standard Contractual Clauses.

Liability:
Liability is capped at 12 months of fees, except for GDPR fines directly caused
by the Processor's proven negligence.

Termination & Return:
On termination, Processor deletes or returns all personal data within 30 days
and certifies deletion.

Governing Law: Germany.

A compliant, low-risk data processing agreement aligned with GDPR.
