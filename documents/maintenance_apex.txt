EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE & SUPPORT AGREEMENT

Parties:
Client: Global Retail AG
Vendor: Apex Infrastructure Services

Effective Date: 22 February 2026
Term: 2 years.

Contract Value: EUR 216,000 (EUR 9,000 per month).

Scope:
Preventive and corrective maintenance of the Client's warehouse automation and
conveyor systems, including spare parts and 24/7 call-out.

Payment Terms:
Net 60 days. Spare parts billed separately at a 20% handling margin.

Service Levels:
4-hour response for critical breakdowns; 95% first-visit fix rate target. Missed
targets trigger a modest credit, but repeated failures carry no strong remedy.

Termination:
Either party may terminate on 90 days' notice. Early termination by the Client
incurs a fee equal to 2 months of service.

Liability:
Capped at 12 months of fees. Vendor excludes liability for production downtime,
which shifts a meaningful operational risk to the Client.

Data Protection (GDPR):
Minimal personal data (technician access logs). GDPR referenced; no full DPA, as
processing is incidental.

Indemnity:
Mutual indemnity for third-party bodily injury or property damage caused by
negligence.

Governing Law: Slovak Republic.

Medium risk: downtime liability excluded, Net 60, early-termination fee and a weak
SLA remedy, offset by a standard liability cap and mutual injury indemnity.
