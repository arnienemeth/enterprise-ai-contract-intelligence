MANAGED HOSTING SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

Parties:
Client: Global Retail AG
Vendor: Nimbus Hosting Ltd

Effective Date: 20 January 2026
Term: 1 year, auto-renewing for successive 1-year terms unless either party gives
60 days' notice.

Contract Value: EUR 4,200 per month (EUR 50,400 per year).

Service Levels:
99.9% monthly uptime guarantee. Support response within 1 hour for critical
incidents. Planned maintenance notified 7 days in advance and performed in a
low-traffic window.

Service Credits:
If uptime falls below target, the Client receives service credits on a published,
capped schedule (maximum 30% of the monthly fee). Credits are the Client's sole
remedy for missed service levels — no punitive penalties.

Payment Terms:
Monthly in arrears, Net 30.

Data Protection (GDPR):
Vendor acts as processor under a signed DPA. All data stored in EU data centers.

Liability:
Aggregate liability capped at the fees paid in the preceding 12 months. Neither
party liable for indirect or consequential loss.

Security:
Vendor maintains ISO 27001 certification, encrypted backups, and 24/7 monitoring.

Termination:
Either party may terminate for uncured material breach after a 30-day cure period.

Governing Law: England and Wales.

A well-balanced, low-risk managed hosting agreement with capped remedies.
