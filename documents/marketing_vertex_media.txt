MARKETING SERVICES AGREEMENT

Parties:
Client: Global Retail AG
Vendor: Vertex Media Group

Effective Date: 10 March 2026
Term: 18 months.

Contract Value: EUR 540,000 (EUR 30,000 per month) plus media spend billed at cost.

Scope:
Performance marketing, paid social, and content production, including management of
the Client's advertising accounts and creative assets.

Payment Terms:
Net 60 days on service fees; media spend prepaid monthly in advance. A 15%
management fee applies on all media spend.

Termination:
Client may terminate on 60 days' notice. Creative work in progress is billed in
full, and prepaid media spend is non-refundable.

Liability:
Capped at 3 months of fees — a relatively low cap given the account access granted.

Data Protection (GDPR):
Vendor processes customer audience data for targeting. A DPA is attached, but the
agreement permits use of aggregated data for the Vendor's own benchmarking, which
warrants review.

Intellectual Property:
Final approved creative transfers to the Client; unused concepts remain the
Vendor's property.

Exclusivity:
Vendor may work with other retailers, including competitors, subject to
confidentiality — a mild conflict-of-interest concern.

Governing Law: Slovak Republic.

Medium risk: low liability cap, non-refundable media spend, competitor exclusivity
gap and secondary data use, balanced by an attached DPA and clean IP transfer.
