CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

Parties:
Client: Global Retail AG
Vendor: Orion Advisory Group LLC

Effective Date: 3 March 2026
Term: 12 months.

Contract Value: EUR 240,000 (EUR 20,000 per month retainer).

Scope:
Strategic advisory on supply-chain digitisation, including workshops, an
assessment report, and a phased implementation roadmap.

Payment Terms:
Net 60 days. A non-refundable mobilisation fee of EUR 40,000 is due on signature.

Termination:
Client may terminate on 60 days' notice. The non-refundable mobilisation fee is
not returned, and any committed third-party costs remain payable.

Liability:
Vendor's liability is capped at fees paid in the preceding 6 months. Vendor
excludes liability for business decisions the Client makes based on the advice.

Data Protection (GDPR):
Limited personal data (employee interview notes) is processed. A short data-
handling clause is included but references GDPR only generally, without a full DPA.

Intellectual Property:
Reports are licensed to the Client for internal use; the Vendor retains ownership
of its underlying methodologies and frameworks.

Confidentiality:
Mutual confidentiality for 3 years.

Governing Law: New York, USA (a non-EU forum, adding some friction for an EU client).

Medium risk: non-refundable fee, Net 60, a light GDPR clause, and a non-EU
governing law, balanced by a reasonable liability cap.
