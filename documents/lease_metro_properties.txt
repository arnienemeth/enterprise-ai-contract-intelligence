COMMERCIAL OFFICE LEASE AGREEMENT

Parties:
Tenant: Global Retail AG
Landlord (Vendor): Metro Properties Ltd

Premises: 4th Floor, Danube Business Center, Bratislava (620 sq m).
Effective Date: 1 March 2026
Term: 5 years, with a mutual break option at year 3 on 6 months' notice.

Contract Value: EUR 18,600 per month (EUR 1,116,000 over the initial term).

Payment Terms:
Rent payable monthly in advance, Net 15. A standard 2-month rent deposit applies,
returned within 30 days of lease end less documented damages.

Rent Review:
Annual indexation capped at 3% or CPI, whichever is lower.

Maintenance & Liability:
Landlord maintains the building structure, common areas, HVAC and elevators.
Landlord carries building insurance. Each party is liable only for damage it
causes; liability for property damage is capped at the insured value.

Data Protection (GDPR):
Building access-control CCTV is operated by the Landlord as controller in line
with GDPR; no tenant personal data is processed by the Landlord beyond this.

Termination:
Standard cure period of 30 days for any breach. Either party may terminate for
uncured material breach. No penalty for the contractual break option.

Governing Law: Slovak Republic.

A conventional, low-risk commercial lease with balanced obligations and
capped, insured liability.
