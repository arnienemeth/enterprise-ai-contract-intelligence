RESELLER & DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

Parties:
Principal: Global Retail AG
Reseller (Vendor): GlobalLink Distribution FZE

Effective Date: 25 February 2026
Term: 3 years, exclusive territory.

Contract Value: Target EUR 2,000,000 in annual resale volume.

Exclusivity & Commitment:
The Reseller receives exclusive distribution rights but commits to aggressive
minimum volumes; failure to meet them still does not let the Principal exit early.

Payment Terms:
Net 150 days. The Reseller may offset alleged marketing costs against amounts due,
creating unpredictable cash flow for the Principal.

Termination:
The Reseller may terminate on 30 days' notice for convenience; the Principal is
locked in for the full term except for the Reseller's uncured insolvency.

Liability:
The Principal's liability is unlimited for any product issue, including indirect
and consequential losses. The Reseller's liability is capped at EUR 25,000.

Indemnity:
The Principal indemnifies the Reseller against all third-party claims, including
those arising from the Reseller's own marketing statements.

Data Protection (GDPR):
Customer lists containing personal data are shared with the Reseller, who is
permitted to reuse them for its other principals — a clear GDPR violation risk.

Governing Law:
Courts of a non-EU free zone, with limited enforcement of EU judgments.

HIGH risk: Net 150 with unilateral offsets, one-sided termination, unlimited
principal liability, sweeping indemnity, and unlawful reuse of personal data.
