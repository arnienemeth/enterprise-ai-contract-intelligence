MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT

Parties:
Buyer: Global Retail AG
Supplier (Vendor): Ironclad Manufacturing Co.

Effective Date: 1 February 2026
Term: 4 years, with a minimum purchase commitment.

Contract Value: EUR 5,400,000 over the term (minimum EUR 1,350,000 per year).

Minimum Commitment:
The Buyer must purchase the annual minimum or pay a shortfall penalty of 25% of
the unmet amount — a significant take-or-pay exposure.

Payment Terms:
Net 180 days from delivery, with no early-payment discount. The Supplier may
change prices with only 15 days' notice.

Termination:
The Supplier may terminate immediately if any single invoice is more than 10 days
overdue. The Buyer may not terminate for convenience during the term.

Liability:
The Supplier caps its liability at 5% of the affected order value and excludes all
liability for defective goods once accepted. The Buyer indemnifies the Supplier
broadly, including for third-party recalls.

Warranty:
A limited 30-day defect window only; no fitness-for-purpose warranty.

Data Protection (GDPR):
Not addressed. Order data includes some personal contact details with no handling
terms.

Force Majeure:
One-sided — protects the Supplier's delivery failures but not the Buyer's payment
obligations.

Governing Law: with mandatory arbitration seated outside the EU.

HIGH risk: Net 180, take-or-pay penalty, immediate supplier termination, a 5%
liability cap, broad buyer indemnity, weak warranty, and no GDPR terms.
