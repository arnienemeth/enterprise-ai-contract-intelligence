MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Parties:
Client: Global Retail AG
Vendor: CloudSphere Solutions Inc.

Effective Date: 15 February 2026
Term: 3 years, auto-renewing for 1-year periods unless 90 days' notice is given.

Contract Value: EUR 960,000 over the initial term (EUR 320,000 per year).

Scope:
Design, development and operation of the Client's e-commerce platform, including
hosting, releases and third-line support.

Payment Terms:
Net 60 days from invoice. Late payments accrue interest at 8% per annum.

Termination:
Client may terminate for convenience on 90 days' notice with payment for work
performed. Either party may terminate for uncured material breach after 30 days.

Liability:
Vendor's aggregate liability is capped at 12 months of fees. However, data-breach
liability is uncapped, which is a point of concern for the Client.

Data Protection (GDPR):
A DPA is referenced but not yet attached. The parties commit to execute it within
30 days of signature. This gap should be closed promptly.

Service Levels:
99.5% uptime with service credits. Auto-renewal and the 90-day exit notice reduce
the Client's flexibility.

Intellectual Property:
Custom deliverables are assigned to the Client; the Vendor retains its pre-existing
tools and libraries under a perpetual license to the Client.

Governing Law: Ireland.

A generally reasonable agreement carrying medium risk: uncapped data-breach
liability, a pending DPA, Net 60 terms, and auto-renewal lock-in.
