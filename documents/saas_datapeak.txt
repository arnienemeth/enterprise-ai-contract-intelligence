SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

Parties:
Client: Global Retail AG
Vendor: DataPeak Analytics Corp.

Effective Date: 8 February 2026
Term: 3 years, auto-renewing for 3-year periods unless 120 days' notice is given.

Contract Value: EUR 780,000 over the initial term.

Payment Terms:
Net 120 days — but the full first year is invoiced and payable in advance. Late
payment interest of 12% per annum applies, and the Vendor may suspend the service
for any overdue amount.

Termination:
The Vendor may terminate or suspend immediately, without notice, for any breach.
The Client has no right to terminate for convenience and forfeits all prepaid fees.

Liability:
The Client's indemnity to the Vendor is uncapped. The Vendor's own liability is
limited to one month of fees. This is a heavily one-sided allocation of risk.

Data Protection (GDPR):
Customer personal data is processed and stored in the United States. No DPA is
attached and no Standard Contractual Clauses are referenced — a serious GDPR gap.

Data Ownership:
The Vendor claims a broad license to use Client data, including for training its
own models, with no clear opt-out.

Service Levels:
No uptime commitment and no service credits are offered.

Governing Law: Delaware, USA, with mandatory arbitration and a waiver of class claims.

HIGH risk: Net 120 with a year prepaid, immediate vendor termination, uncapped
client indemnity, US data transfer with no DPA, broad data reuse, and no SLA.
